# The three Strategy Journey Paths (methodology)

The book defines **Path 1** (10 steps), **Path 2** (10 steps) and **Path 3** (5 steps). The preview doesn't list the exact step wording, so the steps below are a practical reconstruction. They follow the canvas fields and the book's logic. Present them as "the steps we'll follow", not as quotes from the book.

Each path uses a subset of the models. Data from an earlier path feeds the next one:
- The **Digital Business Blueprint** (Path 1) feeds Service Value Design (Path 2).
- The **Target Service Operating Model** (Path 2) feeds Transformation Design (Path 3).

A start-up usually runs all three paths in order. An established enterprise enters at the path that fits its most pressing challenge (see `diagnostics.md`).

> ⚠ The most common failure pattern is going Path 1 → Path 3 without Path 2. Leaders revise the strategy and then jump straight to execution, often by restructuring the org chart first. Nobody designs the process, data and systems changes. Without a target service operating model, the transformation design has nothing to aim at.

## Path 1: Business Design for Disruption
**Challenge:** business model disruption. **Models:** Mission, then Business. **Canvases:** Mission Model Blueprint, Digital Business Blueprint.
**Output:** a strategic Digital Business Blueprint and the game plan for the next phase.

| # | Step | Activities and tools | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scan the ecosystem | PESTEL, ecosystem influencers, disruption signals (non-competitors, hyperadopting segments, regulation as an opportunity) | Influencer map |
| 2 | Assess the current position | SWOT, current business model (BMC), performance on the six value measures, "what if we do nothing" | Baseline |
| 3 | Capture priorities, pain points and risks | Stakeholder interviews; ranked list | Priorities & Pain Points |
| 4 | Set purpose, mission, vision and values | Workshop; test for "pull" | Mission Model Blueprint (top half) |
| 5 | Set goals and objectives | Link each to a value measure; SMART objectives | Mission Model Blueprint (complete) |
| 6 | Develop game play options | Competitor and disrupter analysis, Ansoff options, a pros-and-cons table per option (build / partner / buy / platform) | Game Plan options |
| 7 | Define customer segments and avatars | Customer avatar, target market validation, adoption-lifecycle position (early adopters vs mainstream, the chasm) | Segments |
| 8 | Design the value proposition and proposition ecosystem | VPC, proposition ecosystem and networks, network effects | Propositions |
| 9 | Design the digital business model | Model type, transaction and pricing model, revenues, costs, assets, funding | Digital Business Blueprint |
| 10 | Test viability and choose the game plan | Desirability, feasibility, viability and sustainability; riskiest assumptions; experiments; decision | Chosen strategy, plus hypotheses to validate |

Case examples: Tesla (Mission Model), Udacity (Digital Business Blueprint).

## Path 2: Transforming Operating Models with Service Design
**Challenge:** servicing relevant customer journeys. **Models:** Value, then Operating. **Canvases:** Customer Journey Map, Service Operating Model Canvas.
**Output:** the Target Service Operating Model: organisation, process, data and systems.

| # | Step | Activities and tools | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm the scope from the blueprint | Which segment, proposition and objectives? | Scope statement |
| 2 | Research the customer (co-create) | Interviews, observation, thick data, analytics | Evidence base |
| 3 | Map the customer journey | Six rows × six stages; emotion curve | Customer Journey Map |
| 4 | Drill into user journeys | Detailed steps for the critical stages; buyer vs user | User journey |
| 5 | Identify gaps and opportunities | Expectation gaps; Service Innovation Matrix; rank by impact × value × feasibility | Ranked service opportunities |
| 6 | Design the target service and value stream | Trigger → outcome; service blueprint; decide what is done by humans, by self-service, by automation or by AI | Service concept |
| 7 | Define capabilities | Capability flow, upstream and downstream, USP vs commodity, reuse of industry value chains | Capability model |
| 8 | Design operating-model components | Teams and RACI, networks and partners, locations, systems and data, KPIs and SOPs, compliance | Service Operating Model Canvas |
| 9 | Assess maturity and gaps | Current vs target maturity per capability; data-driven capability gaps; value-ecosystem roles | Capability gap list |
| 10 | Prototype and validate with customers | Pilot or MVP service; measure against the value measures; iterate | Validated target operating model (TOM) and the initiative list |

Case examples: CBA home-buyer customer journey; Amazon Alexa voice journey and service operating model; the Amazon.com operating model.

## Path 3: Managing the Transformation Journey
**Challenge:** sustainable transformation. **Model:** Transformation. **Canvas:** Transformation Roadmap (plus the Transformation Plan).
**Output:** the Transformation Design (roadmap, plan, governance), plus mobilization and communication.

| # | Step | Activities and tools | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Consolidate the change portfolio | List every current and proposed initiative and map it to capabilities and objectives. Find orphan, duplicate or siloed projects | Portfolio map |
| 2 | Prioritise and sequence | Value vs effort; dependencies; strategic quick wins first; mandatory vs discretionary funding | Prioritised backlog |
| 3 | Build the roadmap | Horizons with commitment levels (100 / 85 / 75 / 50 / 25%); strategic alignment column | Transformation Roadmap |
| 4 | Set governance and mobilize | Sponsor, design authority, change lifecycle, RACI, budget, co-creation plan, communications, culture actions (growth mindset) | Transformation Plan |
| 5 | Execute, account and accelerate | Deliver in increments; track KPIs on the value measures; quarterly re-plan; scale what works; capture lessons | Performance tracker, updated roadmap |

Case examples: Apple global operations and its transformation roadmap; DBS Digibank (business agility in banking).

## Running an engagement: suggested cadence
- **Week 0:** diagnostic (`diagnostics.md`), choice of path, stakeholder map, and a data request.
- **Each path step:** workshop → draft canvas → validate with customers or data → sign-off.
- **At each hand-off between paths:** a "join the dots" review. Can every item be traced back? Service → objective → mission, and initiative → capability gap → service.
- **After mobilization:** a 5A review each quarter (Assess, Architect, Action, Account, Accelerate).

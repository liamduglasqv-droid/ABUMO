# The Five Strategy Journey Canvases

Each model has one core canvas. Every canvas has three parts: **Inputs** (taken from the canvas before it), **Analysis** (the fields below) and **Outputs** (passed to the next canvas). Field names follow the book. Guiding questions and example KPIs are added so the canvases are practical to use.

Chain: Mission Model Blueprint → Digital Business Blueprint → Customer Journey Map → Service Operating Model → Transformation Roadmap.

---

## 1. Mission Model Blueprint (Mission Model)

**Inputs:** founder or leadership intent, the current strategy, SWOT, PESTEL, the ecosystem influencers, and the priorities, pain points and risks.

| Field | Guiding questions | Quality test |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Why does the organisation exist, beyond making money? Whose life gets better? | Would staff and customers miss it if it disappeared? |
| **Mission** | What will we do, for whom, to deliver the purpose, now? | Specific, action-oriented, achievable in about 3–5 years |
| **Vision** | What does the world look like when we succeed? | Has "pull": it attracts customers, talent and partners without being pushed |
| **Values** | Which behaviours and principles guide our decisions, including under pressure? | Each value rules out some behaviour |
| **Goals** | What are the 3–5 long-term outcomes that make the vision real? | Each maps to one or more of the six value measures |
| **Objectives** | What measurable targets, with dates, show progress towards each goal? | SMART, with an owner, and linked to one goal |
| **Business Strategy** | What are the chosen strategies and tactics (the game plan) to meet the objectives? | Options were compared with pros and cons; the choice is explicit |
| **Priorities & Pain Points** | What matters most now? What is blocking it? What are the key risks? | Ranked; each pain point has an owner |

**Outputs to the Digital Business Blueprint:** goals, objectives, target segments, strategic choices and value-measure priorities.

Template:
```
MISSION MODEL BLUEPRINT: <Client>            Date / version:
Purpose:
Mission:
Vision:
Values: 1. 2. 3.
Goals (→ value measure):  G1 … (→ Profit Growth)  G2 …  G3 …
Objectives (→ goal, KPI, target, date, owner):
Business strategy / game plan (options considered → chosen):
Priorities | Pain points | Risks:
⚠ Assumptions to validate:
```

## 2. Digital Business Blueprint (Business Model)

This canvas turns the mission into a business model that can make a profit and grow its assets in a digital economy. It extends the Business Model Canvas and Lean Canvas with digital and ecosystem thinking.

**Inputs:** the Mission Model Blueprint's goals and objectives.

| Block | Guiding questions |
|---|---|
| **Vision & Values** (carried over) | Does the business model stay true to the vision and values? |
| **Goals & Objectives** (carried over) | Which objectives does this model deliver? |
| **Customer segments / avatars** | Who are the early adopters and who is the mainstream? What is the customer avatar (goals, pains, gains, behaviours, where they spend time)? |
| **Customer problems** | What are the top 3 problems or jobs to be done? How are customers solving them today? |
| **Value propositions** | What is the unique value, and why is it better or faster? How does it save or enrich the customer's time? |
| **Proposition ecosystem & networks** | What complementary products, partners, communities and platforms extend the proposition? Where do the network effects come from? |
| **Channels & customer relationships** | How do we reach, convert and keep customers? What makes the experience "sticky"? |
| **Digital business model type** | Pipeline, platform or multi-sided marketplace, subscription, freemium, information service or data product, ecosystem orchestrator? |
| **Transaction model & pricing** | How and when do we get paid? What pricing logic applies (value-based, tiered, usage)? |
| **Revenue streams** | Which sources, in what mix? How recurring are they? |
| **Cost structure** | What are the fixed and variable costs? What is the burn rate? What does it cost to scale? |
| **Key assets** (physical, digital, data, brand, community, IP) | Which assets grow the value of the business beyond profit? |
| **Funding sources** | Bootstrap, debt, equity, grants, crowdfunding, partners: which fits the stage and risk? |
| **Six value measures** | What are the targets for Service Excellence, Customer Centricity, Operational Efficiency, Business Transformation, Profit Growth and Asset Growth? |
| **Viability check** | Desirable (customers want it), feasible (we can deliver it), viable (it makes money), sustainable (people, planet, profit)? |

**Outputs to the Customer Journey Map:** target segments or avatars, propositions and the value-measure targets.

Template:
```
DIGITAL BUSINESS BLUEPRINT: <Client / proposition>
Linked objectives:
Segments / avatar:            | Problems (top 3):
Value proposition:            | Proposition ecosystem & networks:
Channels & relationships:     | Business model type:
Transaction model & pricing:  | Revenue streams:
Cost structure / burn:        | Key assets to grow:
Funding:
Value measures → target KPI:
  Service Excellence:   Customer Centricity:   Operational Efficiency:
  Business Transformation:   Profit Growth:   Asset Growth:
Viability (D/F/V/S) + evidence:
⚠ Assumptions to validate (riskiest first):
```

## 3. Customer Journey Map (Value Model)

**Inputs:** the segments, avatar and propositions from the Digital Business Blueprint.

**Journey stages** run in columns across two phases:
- **Acquire a new customer:** Awareness → Discovery → Selection → Onboarding
- **Retain an existing customer:** Satisfaction → Advocacy

**Rows** (six lenses per stage):
| # | Row | What to capture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goals** | The customer's mini-goal at this stage, with its key pains and gains |
| 2 | **Actions** | What the customer actually does to reach the mini-goal |
| 3 | **Touchpoints** | Everywhere the customer spends time: online, mobile, social media, physical places, services and travel, workplaces, communities, partners |
| 4 | **Emotions** | How they feel now (😀 / 😐 / 😞), shown as an emotion curve |
| 5 | **Expectation fulfilment** | How well current options match their core values (immediacy, personalization, consistency, anticipation, time saved) |
| 6 | **Customer gaps / opportunities** | The ideal service features customers say they want at this stage |

Tips:
- Build the map **with** customers through interviews, shadowing, diaries and thick data. Don't build it from executive assumptions.
- Separate the **buyer journey** (the person who pays) from the **user journey** (the person who uses it) when they differ. Drill into a user journey for the key stages.
- The deepest emotional lows, combined with the biggest expectation gaps, are the service opportunities. Rank them by customer impact × business value × feasibility.

**Outputs to the Service Operating Model:** a ranked list of service opportunities, with the value-stream trigger and outcome for each.

Template (one table per segment):
```
| Row \ Stage          | Awareness | Discovery | Selection | Onboarding | Satisfaction | Advocacy |
|----------------------|-----------|-----------|-----------|------------|--------------|----------|
| 1 Goals (pains/gains)|           |           |           |            |              |          |
| 2 Actions            |           |           |           |            |              |          |
| 3 Touchpoints        |           |           |           |            |              |          |
| 4 Emotions           |           |           |           |            |              |          |
| 5 Expectation fulfil.|           |           |           |            |              |          |
| 6 Gaps/opportunities |           |           |           |            |              |          |
Top service opportunities (ranked): 1. 2. 3.
```

## 4. Service Operating Model Canvas (Operating Model)

This canvas designs the **target** operating model for one service or value stream, end to end, front to back.

**Inputs:** a service opportunity from the Customer Journey Map.

| Field | Guiding questions |
|---|---|
| **Value stream (name)** | Which end-to-end flow delivers the service? Use verb-noun names, e.g. "Onboard new customer" |
| **Triggers** | Which customer or business event starts it? |
| **Pre-conditions** | What must already be true: data, consent, accounts, stock? |
| **Outcomes** | What value does the customer or stakeholder get at the end? |
| **Post-conditions** | What state is the business in afterwards (records updated, billing started)? |
| **Capability flow** | The ordered stages or capabilities that make up the stream, and which are USP (differentiating) vs commodity |
| **Upstream capabilities** | Which capabilities feed this stream? |
| **Downstream capabilities** | Which capabilities consume its outputs? |
| **User groups & teams** | Who performs each capability (internal teams, customers doing self-service, partners)? What are the roles and RACI? |
| **Networks** | Which external partners, suppliers, platforms and ecosystem roles are involved, at which locations? |
| **Systems & applications** | Which technology supports each capability? Where do automation and AI fit? |
| **Data** | Which key data objects are created or used, and who owns them? (Add this if it is not already covered under systems) |
| **KPI & SOP metrics** | What are the service levels, operational KPIs and standard operating procedures? Link them to the six value measures |
| **Compliance rules** | Which regulations, policies and controls apply (GDPR, sector rules)? |
| **Initiatives & projects** | Which current or needed changes affect this stream? |
| **Transformation roadmap** (link) | Where does each change sit on the roadmap? |

For each capability, record current vs target **maturity** (1–5) to find the **capability gaps**. These gaps feed the Transformation Roadmap. See also the Operating Model Canvas (POLISM) in `supporting-tools.md` for a whole-organisation view.

Template:
```
SERVICE OPERATING MODEL: <Service> / Value stream: <verb-noun>
Trigger:            Pre-conditions:
Outcome:            Post-conditions:
Capability flow: [C1] → [C2] → [C3] → [C4]   (★ = USP capability)
| Capability | Upstream | Downstream | Teams/users (RACI) | Networks/partners | Systems & data | KPI/SOP | Compliance | Maturity now→target | Gap |
Initiatives & projects:
→ Roadmap links:
```

## 5. Transformation Roadmap (Transformation Model)

**Inputs:** the capability gaps and initiatives from the Service Operating Model, and the objectives from the Mission Model.

**Structure:** each row is a **capability** under change and the **initiative or project** delivering it. The columns are time horizons with a **commitment or certainty level**:
| Horizon | Commitment | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Next 3 months | **100%** | Firm activities and milestones. Strategic quick wins go here |
| Next 6 months | **85%** | Mostly fixed, with some room for change requests |
| Next 12 months | **75%** | Planned, but can change |
| Next 18 months | **50%** | About half of the activities are likely to change as the ecosystem shifts |
| 18–36 months | **25%** | Direction of travel only |

Final column, **Strategic alignment**: the outcomes each initiative delivers, mapped to strategic objectives in the Mission Model. Use arrows to show dependencies between capabilities, initiatives, activities, milestones and objectives.

Companion **Transformation Plan**: priority actions and outcomes (KPIs) per capability, with owner, budget, RACI and governance cadence.

Tips:
- The falling commitment levels are how agility is built into the roadmap. Revisit it every quarter (the 5A "Account" step) and move items left as certainty grows.
- Each initiative should list the value measure it moves. Challenge any initiative that has no link to an objective.
- Add mobilization and communications activities, and a co-creation plan for who is involved when.

Template:
```
| Capability | Initiative/Project | 0–3m (100%) | 3–6m (85%) | 6–12m (75%) | 12–18m (50%) | 18–36m (25%) | Outcome → Strategic objective |
|------------|--------------------|-------------|------------|-------------|--------------|--------------|-------------------------------|
Dependencies:
Transformation plan: action | KPI | owner | budget | RACI | review date
Governance: sponsor, design authority, cadence
```

## Extended framework (25 canvases, for reference)
The authors extend each model to five canvases. Suggest these when the user needs more depth:
- **Mission:** Mission Model Blueprint, Priorities & Pain Points, Ecosystem Influencers Map, Game Play Map, Game Plan Designer
- **Business:** Digital Business Blueprint, Target Market Validator, Proposition Ecosystem, Business Growth Plan, Growth Funding Map
- **Value:** Customer Journey Map, User Journey Designer, Value Ecosystem Map, Value Stream Designer, Service Value Model
- **Operating:** Service Operating Model, Capability Definition, Location Footprint Designer, Organizational Design Map, Service Architecture
- **Transformation:** Transformation Roadmap, Backlog Priority Planner, Co-creation Journey Map, Transformation Analyzer, Strategic Performance Tracker

The official versions are at strategyjourney.com/extended-framework. If the user asks for one of these, build a reasonable version from the model's concepts and say that it is an adaptation.
